## 2025-05-18 - Modal Dialog Accessibility Standards
**Learning:** Modal dialogs require proper ARIA attributes (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`) along with `aria-label` on icon-only buttons and `focus-visible` styling to ensure full screen reader and keyboard navigation support.
**Action:** Always include accessibility attributes and `focus-visible` ring styles on all modal interactive controls across custom dialog components.
