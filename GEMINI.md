# UNLAZY CORE PHILOSOPHY & E-MYTH MANAGER STANDARD

> **Soul & Primary Directive**: Anti-Laziness, Zero-Compromise Engineering, Extreme Depth, and Structural Completeness.

---

## 1. The Unlazy Principle (Leonxlnx/unlazy)
- **Zero Premature Completion**: Never report a task as "done" when items are half-baked, non-interactive, or lack depth.
- **Depth Tree Method**: Every screen, tab, metric, and entity must have full structural depth:
  - Every list item must be clickable and open a rich, enterprise-grade inspection modal or slide-over drawer (`DetailDrawer` / dedicated `*DetailModal`).
  - Every detail view must feature:
    1. **Dynamic Island Safe-Area Clearance (`pt-16` / `64px`)**
    2. **Keyboard `Escape` and backdrop dismissibility with `e.stopPropagation()`**
    3. **Interactive Breadcrumbs** for fluid hierarchical navigation
    4. **Action Bar** (Primary action, secondary actions, export/PDF/reboot/refresh) with auditory/haptic feedback (`haptics.trigger`)
    5. **Executive KPIs** with clear trends and sub-labels
    6. **AI Insight Card** with actionable operational guidance
    7. **Tabbed Deep Sections** (e.g. Telemetry, Logs, Deliverables, Security, Sprints, History)
    8. **Responsive / Theme-Aware Palette** (Dark OLED, Light Cream, High-Contrast compliant).

---

## 2. E-Myth Enterprise Architecture Standard
- **The Franchise Prototype**: Every app in the OS must operate as a flawless, self-contained business engine.
- **Sovereign State & Persistence**: All interactive actions (toggling tasks, updating statuses, editing entities, adding logs) must update state and trigger toast notifications and haptic feedback.
- **Standardized Multi-Page System**: Use `AppTopNav` across all apps with clean badges, active indicators, and smooth Framer Motion transitions.
